### About

I'm a software engineer on the internal engineering team at **Schulte Group** (Bernhard Schulte), one of the world's largest family-owned shipping companies—**140+ years** of history across five generations. The group operates **30+ offices** in major maritime hubs, with a workforce of roughly **45,000** (including a large seafaring population), and a diversified fleet that spans **owned** and **managed** vessels worldwide. I build and evolve the digital tools and systems that support that scale: reliable software, cloud-backed services, and data-informed workflows for a global shipping operation.
